 # Product Roadmap: From Portfolio Tracker to Decision Coach

  ## Summary

  Position the app as a local-first portfolio decision coach. Its advantage over spreadsheets should be continuously evaluating the portfolio, explaining what matters, modeling alternatives, and tracking decisions over
  time.

  The app remains useful with holdings snapshots alone. Optional household data improves planning. It will never connect to brokerage accounts or automatically retrieve statements.

  ## Priority 1 — Decision Coach

  1. Portfolio Review Inbox
      - Rank findings by severity and financial impact.
      - Detect concentration, allocation drift, cash drag, stale prices, missing classifications, currency risk, and inadequate liquidity.
      - Show evidence, importance, confidence, and suggested next steps.

  2. “What Changed?” Snapshot Analysis
      - Compare the latest upload with the previous snapshot.
      - Highlight new and exited holdings, position changes, allocation shifts, cash movements, and unusual valuation changes.
      - Clearly label inferred activity that cannot be distinguished from deposits, withdrawals, or trades.

  3. Scenario Lab
      - Model contributions, withdrawals, reallocations, sales, and cash-reserve changes without modifying stored positions.
      - Compare current and proposed concentration, drift, liquidity, taxes, and goal probability.
      - Save named scenarios for later comparison.

  4. Portfolio Stress Testing
      - Model market crashes, interest-rate shocks, currency moves, sector declines, and security-level shocks.
      - Show projected loss, largest contributors, cash runway, and affected goals.
      - Provide historical-style scenarios using cached public data when available.

  5. Smarter Rebalancing Guidance
      - Prioritize important allocation gaps and estimate their amounts.
      - Prefer contributions and available cash before suggesting sales.
      - Surface tax and holding-period considerations without prescribing exact trades.

  6. Look-Through Exposure and Overlap
      - Identify duplicate exposure across funds and directly held securities.
      - Report effective company, sector, geography, currency, and asset-class concentration.
      - Show metadata freshness and support manually supplied fund holdings.

  7. Risk Decomposition
      - Estimate volatility, drawdown, correlation, beta, and holding-level risk contributions.
      - Distinguish large positions from large sources of risk.
      - Warn when historical data is insufficient.

  8. Fees and Portfolio Drag
      - Track expense ratios, advisory fees, account fees, and idle cash.
      - Estimate annual and long-term costs.
      - Support manual metadata when public data is unavailable.

  ## Priority 2 — Wealth Planning

  9. Goal-Linked Portfolio Buckets
      - Assign accounts or portions of holdings to goals.
      - Measure funding, risk suitability, liquidity, and probability of success.
      - Identify assets allocated to multiple goals.

  10. Household Balance Sheet
      - Add mortgages, loans, interest rates, payments, and maturities.
      - Calculate liquid net worth, debt ratios, reserves, and payoff scenarios.
      - Keep household information encrypted locally.

  11. Cash-Flow and Runway Planner
      - Support income, expenses, contributions, deposits, withdrawals, and planned large expenses.
      - Project liquidity and goal funding under baseline and adverse scenarios.
      - Use manual portfolio cash flows to improve performance calculations.

  12. Retirement and Withdrawal Planning
      - Model accumulation, drawdown, inflation, contribution changes, and sequence risk.
      - Compare retirement dates, spending levels, and withdrawal strategies.
      - Present ranges and failure probabilities rather than deterministic forecasts.

  13. Tax Opportunity Watchlist
      - Identify potential loss-harvesting candidates, approaching long-term dates, embedded gains, and estimated exposure.
      - Make jurisdiction and tax assumptions explicit.
      - Remain informational and avoid filing instructions.

  ## Priority 3 — Long-Term Differentiation

  14. Decision Journal
      - Record theses, expected outcomes, review dates, and rationale.
      - Compare decisions with subsequent snapshots.
      - Separate process quality from short-term outcomes.

  15. Local Alerts and Review Cadence
      - Generate local alerts for concentration, drift, drawdown, liquidity, stale data, and goal-probability thresholds.
      - Support monthly and quarterly review checklists.
      - Avoid background brokerage connectivity.

  16. Data Quality Center
      - Centralize missing cost basis, stale snapshots, unresolved securities, suspect FX rates, duplicates, and incomplete assumptions.
      - Attach confidence scores to affected analytics.

  17. Personalized Portfolio Report
      - Generate a local PDF or HTML review covering changes, risks, goals, scenarios, and follow-ups.
      - Include assumptions and data-quality limitations.

  ## Core Interfaces

  - Use a common InsightFinding model containing category, severity, impact, confidence, evidence, explanation, and suggested actions.
  - Store non-destructive scenarios containing proposed cash flows, position changes, and allocation changes.
  - Add encrypted household assumptions, liabilities, manual cash flows, alert thresholds, and journal records.
  - Cache token-free public market and reference data locally with timestamps and offline/manual fallbacks.
  - Never present inferred trades, returns, exposures, or tax estimates as confirmed facts.

  ## Validation

  - Test concentrated, diversified, cash-heavy, multi-currency, incomplete, and single-holding portfolios.
  - Confirm scenarios never mutate actual holdings.
  - Test stale/unavailable public data and complete offline operation.
  - Ensure snapshot comparisons distinguish confirmed changes from inference.
  - Disclose missing-data limitations in risk, tax, performance, and goal outputs.
  - Confirm household and planning data remains inside the encrypted database.

  ## Assumptions

  - Decision support comes before deeper wealth planning.
  - Recommendations explain and suggest rather than prescribe exact trades.
  - Holdings snapshots remain the primary input.
  - Household details and portfolio cash flows are optional and manually entered.
  - Token-free public data is permitted with caching and offline fallbacks.
  - Brokerage credentials, statement APIs, token-based ingestion, and automatic statement retrieval remain out of scope.
